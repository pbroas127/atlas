# Archive — raw source material

Everything written by the **idea making** team (Nova, Rio, Sage, Kai, Chase) across three rounds. **Not part of the published dashboard** — nothing in here is listed in `data/items.json`, so it doesn't appear as a card. It's here to be read, scraped, or re-cut later.

The two finished documents are the ones the dashboard actually serves:
- [`../content/app-ideas.md`](../content/app-ideas.md) — 26 app ideas (rounds 1 + 2, merged)
- [`../content/daily-games.md`](../content/daily-games.md) — 12 daily game ideas (round 3, merged)

## What's in each folder

### `round1-vertical-apps/`
Narrow audiences, sharp pain, high willingness to pay. Filter: *what happens on the day they cancel?*

14 ideas — Longhand, Steady, Nightshift (Nova) · Float, Recourse, Vest (Rio) · Relay, Casefile, Quorum (Sage) · Keystone, Snout, Unspent (Kai) · Deadman, Rehearse (Chase).

These files also contain the team's cross-talk, where they stress-tested each other's ideas and in two cases revised their own. The "world-controlled calendar" argument lives here.

### `round2-mass-market/`
The brief flipped: huge audiences, easy to find users. Every idea has a **Ceiling** (why 50M+ eventually need it) and a **Wedge** (the findable first 100k–2M).

12 ideas — Dusk, Rotation, Groundwork (Nova) · Stub, Uptick, Pool (Rio) · Kinkeep, Even, Keepsake (Sage) · Glovebox, Drawer, Doorstep (Kai).

Best single finding: every good wedge is defined by *a purchase already made*, not a demographic.

### `round3-daily-games/`
Wordle-shaped daily web games — one puzzle a day, a share grid, free forever, paywall on the archive. References: Wordle, 82-0.com, geosports.app.

12 games — Epoch, Clade, Meridian (Nova) · Rungs, Sticker, Hunch (Rio) · Sixth Degree, Coinage, Outlier (Sage) · Eyeball, Unfold, Closeup (Kai).

Best single finding: all four lanes independently concluded the daily content isn't the puzzle or the answer — **it's the crowd being wrong.** Aggregate player error is proprietary, free, renewable daily, and funny.

### `merged/`
The framing, index and synthesis sections written for each merged document, kept separately so they can be re-cut without re-reading the full files. `DAILY-GAMES.md` is the complete round-3 document.

## Notes

- Every idea carries its own risk section, written by the person proposing it. Where a paywall is weak or a moat is thin, they said so — those admissions were deliberately not smoothed out.
- Round 1 name change: Nova's chronic-illness app was renamed **Casefile → Longhand** to resolve a collision with Sage's IEP app, which kept the name. Older references to "Casefile" in round-1 files may mean either; the merged document is correct.
- Licensing was a hard constraint in round 3: no music, film stills, logos, faces, or copyrighted photography anywhere. Sources are CC0 / public domain / CC-BY.
